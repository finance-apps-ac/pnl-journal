Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Tradeology Account

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and its data for Tradeology: Learn to Trade, provided by Anna Cardoso. An account is optional – Tradeology works without one – and you can delete it at any time.

Delete directly in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tradeology and tap the profile icon (top right of the home screen) to open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account…
  3. Tap Delete forever to confirm. Your account and its data are deleted immediately and permanently.
Your Tradeology account is a FinanceLog account – the same sign-in used by our P&L Trading Journal and Budget apps. Deleting it removes the account and all data on it, including any P&L or Budget data on the same email.

Or request deletion by email

If you can’t access the app, email support@financelog.app from the email address on your account with the subject line “Delete my account.” We will verify the request, delete your account and data within 30 days, and confirm by email when it’s done.

What is deleted

  • Your account credentials (email address and password hash) and your account ID.
  • Your profile photo, if you added one.
  • Your synced learning progress – completed lessons, quiz results, XP and day streak.
  • Any settings linked to your account.

Progress saved only on your device stays there until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

What is kept, and for how long

We keep no copy of your account data after deletion. Removal from our live database (Supabase) is immediate; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged on our provider’s rolling backup cycle within 30 days. Subscription purchase records are held by Apple or Google under their own policies, not by us. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Contact

Questions about deleting your account or your data? Email support@financelog.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
