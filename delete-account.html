Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data for P&L – Stock Trading Journal and Budget – Annual Tracker, the apps provided by Anna Cardoso. Your financial data is yours, and you can remove it at any time.

Delete directly in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear / menu icon).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately and permanently.

Or request deletion by email

If you can’t access the app, email support@financelog.app from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. Please use the subject line “Delete my account.” We will verify your request and delete your account and data within 30 days, and confirm by email once it’s done.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account credentials (email address and password hash).
  • All data you entered into the app — trades, holdings, contributions, budget figures, and any notes.
  • Any settings and preferences linked to your account.

What is kept, and for how long

We do not retain a copy of your account data after deletion. Deletion from our live database (Supabase) is immediate; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged on our provider’s rolling backup cycle within 30 days. We keep no personal data beyond that period. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising at any time.

Contact

Questions about account deletion or your data? Email support@financelog.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
